Our Flier Products

📬

A6/DL Flyers

Small, compact flyers perfect for high-volume distribution and hand-outs. These pocket-sized leaflets are ideal for marketing teams looking to reach a wide audience cost-effectively. Easy to carry and distribute at events, stores, or door-to-door campaigns.

📄

A5 Flyers

Medium-sized flyers offering more space for detailed information without being bulky. A5 flyers are the most popular choice for their balance between content capacity and distribution ease. Perfect for showcasing products, events, or special promotions.

📋

A4 Flyers

Full-page flyers providing maximum space for comprehensive details, images, and branding elements. A4 flyers work best for in-depth product information, event details, or professional announcements where visual impact is crucial.

📑

Tri-Fold Brochures

Professional three-panel layout designed for detailed storytelling and premium presentation. Tri-fold brochures are ideal for companies, tourism, or services needing organized, structured information display with impressive visual appeal.

🎫

Perforated Fliers

Flyers with tear-off coupons, contact info tabs, or promotional codes. Perfect for creating actionable marketing materials that drive customer response. Recipients can easily detach contact information or save discount coupons for later use.

✂️

Custom Die-Cut Fliers

Unique, eye-catching shapes tailored to your brand or campaign theme. Custom die-cut fliers stand out from standard rectangular formats and create memorable impressions. Perfect for special events, product launches, or creative marketing initiatives.

Flier Types

  • Half-Page Fliers: Compact & easy to distribute
  • Full-Page Fliers: More space for detailed information
  • Tri-Fold Brochures: Professional three-part layout
  • Bi-Fold Fliers: Two-panel design for events
  • Custom Shapes: Die-cut designs for unique appeal
  • Perforated Fliers: Tear-off coupons and contact info
