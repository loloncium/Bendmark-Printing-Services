About our 2D Signs

Clean, professional flat signage that communicates your message clearly and affordably. Our 2D signs are versatile and fast to produce, making them a smart choice for offices, shopfronts, institutions, and outdoor branding.

From acrylic and ACP signboards to vinyl lettering and window graphics, we use premium materials and sharp, full-colour printing or precision cutting. Built to withstand the weather while keeping your branding crisp for years to come.

Features & Benefits

  • Sharp, clear and professional finish
  • Durable, weather-resistant materials
  • Custom sizing, colours and finishes
  • Fast turnaround compared to dimensional signage
  • Budget-friendly for any project size
  • Professional installation support
  • Excellent for offices, storefronts & institutions

Production Timeline

  • Design Phase: 1–2 business days
  • Production: 2–5 business days
  • Installation: 1 business day
  • Rush Orders: Available (additional fees apply)
  • Total Average Time: 3–7 days

Our Design Process

Our expert design team will work with you to create the perfect 2D sign:

  • Consultation: Discuss your message, size and placement
  • Artwork Design: Layouts, colours and typography drafted for you
  • Proof Approval: Review and sign off before production
  • Production: Precision printing and cutting in-house
  • Quality Control: Careful inspection of every piece
  • Delivery & Installation: Professional setup at your location
